    Smart contract audit & verification

    From vulnerable
    to verifiable.

    VULNEX takes a Solidity contract through the whole audit in one workspace: rule-based detection, highlighted evidence, plain-language explanations, suggested fixes, re-scans, a before-and-after comparison, and an on-chain record anyone can check.

    Prototype build. The rules run in your browser; attestations are recorded on Ethereum Sepolia through MetaMask.

    Seven steps, one workspace

    Each step feeds the next, so the record at the end describes exactly what was found, what changed and what was checked.

    1. 01

      Find

      Deterministic rules flag selected vulnerability classes, each with a severity.

    2. 02

      Understand

      The exact lines are highlighted and every finding is explained in plain language.

    3. 03

      Fix

      A suggested patch appears as a diff. You review it before it touches your code.

    4. 04

      Re-test

      The new version is scanned with the same rules.

    5. 05

      Compare

      See what was resolved, what remains and what is new.

    6. 06

      Certify

      Report and contract are hashed, and the hashes attested on-chain.

    7. 07

      Verify

      Anyone with the Audit ID re-hashes the report and checks it against the record.

    Each layer has one job

    AI proposes. Evidence supports. Security rules validate. Blockchain preserves the proof.

    Rules

    Detect

    Static rules decide what counts as a finding. They are the security authority.

    AI

    Explain and suggest

    Claude explains findings and drafts fixes. It never decides what is safe; the rules re-check every change.

    Hash

    Fingerprint

    SHA-256 over the canonical report JSON and over the exact contract source.

    Chain

    Preserve

    attest(auditId, reportHash, contractHash) stores two hashes. Never the source, never the report.

    What the rules look for

    Selected classes only, by design. Every rule maps to a known weakness pattern.

    FamilyRuleSeverityReference

    What a verified badge means

    Attestation makes the record tamper-evident. It is not a security guarantee, and the interface says so wherever a result appears.

    We do not claim

    • AI guarantees smart contract security.
    • Blockchain proves the contract is safe.
    • The scanner detects every vulnerability.

    We do claim

    • AI helps developers understand findings and fixes.
    • The audit record is tamper-evident and independently verifiable.
    • The rules find selected classes of vulnerabilities, and the workflow connects find, fix, re-test, certify and verify.

    A verified attestation proves that this audit record and contract version match what was recorded at that time. It does not prove that the contract is secure.
